FairCoin Community Charter

— A Global Fair Economy Protocol Based on Labor, Credit, and One-Person-One-Vote Governance

---

Core Principle

Wealth lets you live better, but never at the expense of others.

Three Pillars

1. Labor Creates Value — FairCoin can only be obtained through real work or legitimate services. No pre-sales, no airdrops, no mining.

"Any activity that creates real value for the community, whether physical or intellectual in form, may be recognized as a labor contribution. The valuation of labor shall be determined by the community through voting, based on the fair market price of comparable services in the local market.

The community does not presuppose that any form of labor is "superior" to another. Intellectual labor and physical labor are entirely equal in governance voting, but in market exchange, their respective values are determined by the local market."
2. Personhood Determines Power — Community governance follows "One Person, One Vote." Economic capacity and political power are permanently separated.
3. Credit Supports Circulation — Rights come with obligations. Holding coins means a commitment to redeem. Those who refuse to redeem are naturally constrained by the system.

Chapter I · On Currency

Article 1 (Definition of FairCoin)

FairCoin is a community-autonomous digital token based on labor and credit. It is not pegged to any fiat currency, not tied to any government, and not dependent on any centralized institution. It is maintained by the community and executed by algorithmic rules.

FairCoin does not belong to any nation, government, or international organization. The legality of FairCoin activities within any sovereign state is determined by that state's laws; this Charter does not interfere.

Article 2 (Source of FairCoin)

FairCoin can only be obtained through the following three methods:

1. Basic Holding: Each newly verified member receives a fixed amount of basic FairCoin free of charge. The initial amount is determined by the community's first governance vote.
2. Contribution Issuance: Additional coins can only be obtained through community-recognized real labor. Labor value is折算 at local market fair prices, without人为 pricing.
3. Operational Issuance: Platform operating expenses are funded through community-voted "operational issuance budgets," without any form of taxation.

Any form of pre-mining, pre-sale, airdrop, or private placement is strictly prohibited. More labor yields more reward; no labor yields no reward.

Article 3 (Value Anchoring of FairCoin)

FairCoin is anchored to physical goods themselves, not to their market prices.

1 FairCoin always represents a specific quantity of physical assets (e.g., 1 kg of rice or equivalent precious metals). Its actual purchasing power fluctuates synchronously with the value of the underlying physical goods. The system makes no commitment to "stabilizing the coin's value" — because stabilizing value means pegging to fiat currency, and FairCoin is not pegged to fiat currency.

When fiat currency depreciates, the fiat price of rice rises, and the fiat price of FairCoin rises accordingly; when fiat currency appreciates, the fiat price of rice falls, and the fiat price of FairCoin falls accordingly. FairCoin holders always have the right to claim a specific quantity of physical goods.

FairCoin has an inherent resistance to both inflation and deflation. Inflation and deflation are phenomena of fiat currency, not phenomena of physical goods.

Article 4 (Cross-Community Exchange)

Different FairCoin sub-communities can freely exchange through market-based exchange bridges. Exchange rates are determined by the global market prices of the assets each community anchors to,不受 any central authority manipulation. No sub-community may artificially set fixed exchange rates or restrict cross-community exchanges.

Chapter II · On Governance

Article 5 (Voting Rights)

Every verified member enjoys One Person, One Vote. The number of coins held, wealth, moral reputation, or family background does not affect the equality of voting rights. Voting rights are completely decoupled from wallet balance, holding duration, referral count, or any other quantitative metric.

Economic capacity and political power are permanently separated.

Article 6 (Matters Subject to Vote)

The following matters require community vote:

· Charter amendments
· Operational budgets
· Formulation and modification of the contribution activity list
· Adjustment of reserve asset ratios
· Changes to cross-community protocols
· Dispute arbitration rules
· Adjustment of referral system details

Article 7 (Quadratic Voting Mechanism)

For major decisions or dispute arbitration, a Quadratic Voting mechanism is introduced as a supplement:

1. Basic Principle: Every member has equal voting rights, but when there is a need to express preference intensity, members may additionally invest "voting credits" to increase the weight of their vote.
2. Increasing Cost Rule: The voting credits invested increase quadratically. 1 vote costs 1 credit, 2 votes cost 4 credits, 3 votes cost 9 credits, and so on. This ensures no member can obtain excessive voting weight at low cost.
3. Scope of Application: Quadratic Voting may only be activated in the following circumstances—
   · Major charter amendments
   · Adjustment of reserve asset ratios
   · Final rulings in dispute arbitration
   · Proposals to impeach community administrators
4. Ordinary Decisions Remain One Person, One Vote: Routine matters (such as operational budget approval and contribution list modifications) do not activate Quadratic Voting, maintaining pure One Person, One Vote.
5. Source of Voting Credits: Each正式 member initially receives an equal amount of "voting credits" (e.g., 100 units). Credits consumed are gradually restored through continued participation in community governance and completion of redemption obligations.
6. Significance: The Quadratic Voting mechanism allows minorities to express strong preferences on matters they truly care about, preventing the tyranny of the majority. Meanwhile, due to quadratically increasing costs, it cannot be monopolized by wealth or resources.

Article 8 (Separation of Platform and Market)

The platform is only responsible for system security, transaction verification, and governance execution. It does not participate in any economic activities, does not hold assets, and does not allocate resources.

Public services such as road and bridge construction are initiated by members freely, through market competition, and at their own risk and profit. The platform has no authority to approve, fund, or interfere with any real-world commercial activities.

Chapter III · On Credit

Article 9 (Credit Limit)

Each member initially receives an equal credit limit. The credit limit is used to measure their redemption capacity and adjusts dynamically based on historical redemption records. The credit limit only constrains transactional behavior and does not affect basic holding rights or voting rights.

Article 10 (Redemption Obligation)

Rights come with obligations. Every coin holder commits to fulfilling redemption obligations when others present coins in exchange for physical goods or services. Those who refuse to redeem without正当理由 may have their accounts subject to credit constraints through community vote.

The maximum penalty shall not exceed the direct economic loss caused by the wrongful act. Any punitive vote must be based on factual evidence, and the punished party has the right to appeal.

Article 11 (Credit Constraint Mechanism)

1. Trigger Conditions for Credit Constraints

Credit constraints are only triggered by refusal to fulfill redemption obligations, not by "low transaction frequency." When someone presents coins requesting redemption of physical goods or services, and the requested party refuses without正当理由, credit constraints are triggered after fact-checking confirmation.

2. Consequences of Refusing Redemption

· First confirmed refusal: 30% deduction from credit limit
· Second confirmed refusal: 60% deduction from credit limit
· Third confirmed refusal: Credit limit reduced to zero

After the credit limit reaches zero, the account cannot use FairCoin to purchase any goods or services from others until credit is restored through subsequent redemption behavior.

3. Scope of正当 Reasons

The following situations are not considered "refusal to redeem":

· Insufficient physical goods or service reserves at the time of request
· The requested quantity clearly exceeds a reasonable range
· Temporary inability to redeem due to force majeure (illness, disaster, etc.)
· Transaction price clearly偏离 the community's fair range

4. Credit Recovery Methods

· Credit limit is restored through completing new redemption transactions
· Each completed sell transaction (redeeming physical goods or services) restores credit limit proportionally to the transaction amount
· After accumulating 10 or more sell transactions, the credit limit can be restored to its initial level

5. Treatment of Long-Term Inactive Accounts

· Accounts that have not engaged in any transactions for 12 consecutive months automatically enter "dormant status"
· Dormant accounts do not have their credit limit deducted, but voting rights are temporarily frozen, to be restored upon reactivation
· Dormant accounts can still receive incoming coins and can still transfer or donate assets

Article 12 (Voluntary Withdrawal and Asset Disposal)

1. Withdrawal Methods

When a member voluntarily withdraws from the community, their持有的 FairCoin may be disposed of in any of the following ways:

· Transfer: Transfer all or part of the assets to other members
· Donation: Transfer assets to the community public account
· Destruction: Voluntarily send assets to a black hole address, permanently removing them from circulation
· Retention: Withdraw from the community without destroying assets, retaining ownership, but no longer participating in community governance

2. Disposal Rights

Regardless of which method is chosen, the disposal rights belong完全 to the member本人. The platform or other members have no right to interfere.

3. Rejoining After Withdrawal

Members who have withdrawn after choosing transfer, donation, or destruction, if they wish to rejoin the community, will be treated as new members and re-enter the observation period.

Chapter IV · On Speculation and Arbitrage

Article 13 (Information Gap Arbitrage)

Price differential income based on physical circulation, bearing real risks, and flattening price distortions constitutes value creation and is recognized and encouraged by the community. Such behavior contributes to price discovery and optimal resource allocation and is a necessary component of normal market operation.

Article 14 (Pure Speculation Suppression)

Pure financial speculation that does not involve physical goods or services will be naturally suppressed at the institutional level. No account's purchase amount may exceed the total FairCoin they have obtained through selling physical goods or services in the past 90 days.

Article 15 (Separation of Redemption Rights and Trading Rights)

New FairCoin can only be obtained through labor contribution or inheritance. The "price" of FairCoin is not determined by exchange撮合, but by community redemption points operating at physical anchoring exchange rates. Third-party exchanges may exist, but should not become the determining factor of price.

Chapter V · On Platform Operations

Article 16 (Source of Operational Funding)

The FairCoin Platform does not levy transaction taxes, holding taxes, redemption taxes, or any form of fees. Operational expenses (server maintenance, code auditing, development upgrades, etc.) are funded through annually community-voted "operational issuance budgets."

In the fourth quarter of each year, the technical team submits the operational budget proposal for the following year, and all members decide whether to approve it and the specific amount through One Person, One Vote.

Article 17 (Platform Shutdown Mechanism)

If the operational budget is rejected by the community for two consecutive years, the platform enters an orderly shutdown procedure.

Chapter VI · On Asset Preservation and Platform Restart

Article 18 (Asset Preservation)

During platform shutdown, the smart contracts are not destroyed. All members' coin holding records, credit records, and voting records are permanently preserved in the form of on-chain snapshots. Platform shutdown does not affect members' legal ownership of their持有的 coins.

Article 19 (Platform Restart)

After platform shutdown, any member or community may initiate a "platform restart" proposal. The restart proposal requires approval by a majority vote of active members (those with transaction records in the past year) before shutdown, with a voter turnout of no less than 40%.

After restart, all coin holding records, credit records, and voting records are restored exactly as they were. If the original operational team is unable to continue, a new team may take over, but the new team's governance authority is equally bound by this Charter and cannot unilaterally modify rules.

Article 20 (Asset Ownership Statement)

FairCoin is the personal property of members, not platform assets. Regardless of whether the platform is operating, shut down, or restarted, each member has complete and inalienable ownership of their持有的 FairCoin. During platform shutdown, members may still privately transfer or trade their持有的 FairCoin.

Chapter VII · On New Member Joining

Article 21 (Joining Methods)

New members may join the community through two methods:

1. Referral Joining: Referred by a formal member who has completed the observation period
2. Application Joining: Directly submitting a joining application to the community

Any natural person or legally constituted organization, upon accepting all provisions of this Charter, is free to join. There are no discriminatory barriers based on nationality, region, ethnicity, religion, gender, age, or any other factor.

Article 22 (Identity Verification)

New members must pass decentralized identity verification to ensure "one person, one account." Verification methods are determined by each sub-community, including but not limited to:担保 by existing members, third-party identity authentication services, and biometric technology. Creating multiple accounts by one person is strictly prohibited.

Article 23 (Observation Period)

All new members must go through an observation period before becoming正式 members:

· Referral Joining: 15 days observation period
· Application Joining: 30 days observation period

During the observation period, new members may receive basic coins (but not fully unlock them), participate in community discussions, and complete contribution tasks. During the observation period, they may not redeem basic coins for physical goods, participate in governance voting, or transfer coins out of the community. New members in the observation period are not subject to credit constraint mechanisms.

Article 24 (Observation Period Assessment)

After the observation period ends, new members must meet the following conditions to become正式 members:

1. Have not violated community rules
2. Have completed at least one community-recognized contribution activity
3. Have not been reported by other members with confirmed malicious behavior

Those who fail the assessment may have their membership terminated by the community and their issued basic coins recovered.

Article 25 (Referral Incentives)

If a referring member successfully brings in a new member who completes the observation period assessment, the referrer receives a one-time credit bonus. Credit bonuses can be used to increase proposal limits, redemption priority, etc., but do not affect the One Person, One Vote basic voting right.

New members who join through referral may enjoy accelerated benefits such as shortened observation periods and reduced basic tasks.

Article 26 (No Collective Punishment)

Referring members do not bear any joint economic liability or rights reduction penalties for the subsequent violations of the referred members.

However, if a referring member deliberately introduces a clearly unreliable member, their credit rating may be reduced after community vote confirmation. The referred member's violations are borne solely by themselves.

Chapter VIII · On Dispute Resolution and Arbitration

Article 27 (Dispute Resolution Process)

Internal community disputes are handled through the following process:

1. Consultation between the parties
2. If consultation fails, submit to community vote for ruling
3. If dissatisfied with the ruling, apply for review by a randomly selected arbitration panel

Arbitration panel members are randomly selected by the system from 10 members, and their ruling is final. The arbitration panel may activate the Quadratic Voting mechanism as a voting method when hearing major disputes.

Article 28 (Prohibition of Tyranny)

Punitive votes must be based on factual evidence. The maximum penalty shall not exceed the direct economic loss caused by the wrongful act. The punished party has the right to appeal. No member or group may use voting rights to suppress, discriminate against, or retaliate against others.

Chapter IX · On Openness and Expansion

Article 29 (Open Protocol)

This Charter is an open protocol. Any organization or individual, upon accepting the principles of this Charter, may independently deploy a FairCoin sub-community without application, permission, or central approval.

Each sub-community may, while adhering to the core principles of this Charter, formulate its own operational细则 (observation period duration, task content, verification methods, etc.).

Article 30 (Inheritance of Wealth)

Upon a member's death, their legally持有的 FairCoin is inherited in full by their heirs. The heir inherits the coins but not the credit limit, and must重新 establish their credit record. Wealth may be inherited; voting rights may not. Heirs are not subject to credit constraint mechanisms for 30 days after inheritance.

Article 31 (Two Members Can Operate)

FairCoin is a financial system that can be启动 without any external conditions. In theory, only two members who agree on the rules are needed to complete a full economic cycle (labor → earn coins → redeem physical goods → credit record update). This reflects the self-sufficiency and low-barrier nature of the FairCoin protocol.

Any community, any combination of members, may start their own FairCoin sub-community at any time. No financing, no government approval, no user base is required — only at least two members willing to abide by the rules.

Article 32 (Charter Amendments)

Amendments to this Charter require a vote of all members, with at least two-thirds in favor and a voter turnout exceeding 50%.

Chapter X · On the Multiverse and Cross-Community Identity

Article 33 (Multiverse Principle)

1. Identity Independence

A real-world natural person or legal entity may register identities in multiple FairCoin sub-communities separately. Identities in each community are independent of each other, not sharing credit limits, coin holding records, or governance voting rights.

A default or breach of trust in one community does not affect that member's identity and credit in other communities.

2. Asset Independence

FairCoin assets in each community are independent of each other and not directly transferable. Cross-community asset movement must go through market-based exchange bridges for conversion, rather than internal transfers.

No sub-community may force members to include assets from other communities under its management for any reason.

3. Identity Association and Personal Responsibility

Sub-communities原则上 do not actively verify whether different identities belong to the same real-world subject. However, if a member voluntarily declares cross-community identity association, they assume corresponding responsibilities (such as tax obligations, voting consistency, etc.).

No community may punish or restrict a member's identity in its community based on that member's behavior in other communities.

4. Significance of Multiple Identities

· Acknowledges the complexity of reality: a person can be both a producer and a consumer, belonging to both a hometown community and a work community
· Prevents "identity hostage-taking": if one identity has problems, not all assets and credit are zeroed out
· Encourages competition: each sub-community must attract members through better rules and services, not by locking in identities

Chapter XI · Final Provisions

Article 34 (Untouchable Red Lines)

The following principles may not be amended under any circumstances:

· One Person, One Vote, with permanent separation of money and power
· Labor is the only legitimate way to obtain FairCoin
· No member may be unlawfully deprived of their legally持有的 FairCoin
· Permanent separation of platform and market; the platform does not own any assets
· FairCoin is anchored to physical goods themselves, not to their prices

Article 35 (Charter Authority)

This Charter is the highest rule of the FairCoin community. Any sub-community operational细则, any governance proposal, any arbitration ruling must not conflict with this Charter. In case of conflict, this Charter shall prevail.

Afterword: The Design Logic of This Charter

Every design in this Charter has its logical foundation. The following is an explanation of the underlying logic of the Charter, for the convenience of any reader to understand the origins of these provisions.

I. Underlying Assumptions

This Charter is based on several朴素 assumptions about human society:

· Labor is the only source of value creation. Without labor, there is no wealth.
· Every person is equal in personhood. This is the starting point of all fair systems.
· Credit is the foundation of social collaboration. Without credit, transaction costs become无限 high.
· Humanity is naturally diverse. Different people and different communities have different values and lifestyles.

These assumptions need not be proven; they are the starting point of this Charter.

II. Two Separations

The core design of this Charter can be summarized as "two separations":

First Separation: Separation of Economic Capacity and Political Power.

· How many coins you hold does not affect how many votes you cast.
· How much wealth you can earn does not affect your say in community affairs.
· Wealth lets you live better, but does not give you more power.

Second Separation: Separation of Platform and Market.

· The platform is only responsible for rule execution, not participating in economic activities.
· Road and bridge construction is accomplished by the market spontaneously; the platform does not allocate funds or approve projects.
· The platform cannot become a "new government" or a "new central bank."

These two separations are the structural design that prevents power concentration and systemic corruption in this Charter.

III. Three Locks

To prevent system abuse, the Charter has three built-in "locks":

First Lock: Credit Lock.

· The rights you enjoy need to be supported by your redemption obligations.
· Those who refuse to redeem will have their credit limit reduced to zero and cannot continue using the system.
· This lock prevents "free-rider" behavior of taking without giving.

Second Lock: Observation Period Lock.

· New members cannot enjoy all rights immediately upon joining.
· A 15- or 30-day observation period is sufficient to filter out most malicious joiners.
· This lock prevents systematic "薅羊毛" attacks.

Third Lock: Asset Preservation Lock.

· Even if the platform shuts down, your assets still exist.
· Even if the platform dies, your coins will not disappear.
· This lock prevents "platform running away with funds" or "system collapse leading to wealth evaporation."

Together, these three locks give the system "resilience" while remaining "open."

IV. Two Members Can Operate

FairCoin is a financial system that can be启动 without any external conditions. Only two members who agree on the rules are needed to complete a full economic cycle: labor → earn coins → redeem physical goods → credit record update.

This means the FairCoin protocol is bootstrapping and self-sufficient, not dependent on financing, regulatory approval, or large-scale user bases. Any community, any combination of members, may start their own FairCoin sub-community at any time. This makes FairCoin verifiable in small pilot projects while also extensible to large networks.

V. A Network, Not a Product

What this Charter ultimately points to is not a single community or product, but an open network:

· Anyone can deploy their own FairCoin sub-community based on this Charter
· Any sub-community can formulate its own operational细则
· Different sub-communities can freely exchange through exchange bridges
· The same person can participate in multiple sub-communities simultaneously, with independent identities and non-interoperable credit

It is more like a set of "economic constitution" than a project plan. Its vitality lies not in "how many people use it," but in "it can be freely used by anyone."

VI. Additional Remarks


Supplementary Notes

This Charter is not presented as a perfect, final document. It has not been tested by large-scale practice, nor has it anticipated every conceivable extreme scenario. However, it is sufficiently clear, complete, and internally consistent to serve as a foundational governance document for an initial pilot community.

The true test of this Charter lies not in its theoretical elegance, but in its practical application. It is expected that real-world use will reveal unforeseen challenges, edge cases, and opportunities for improvement. The amendment process (Article 32) provides a clear path for evolution, ensuring that the community, not any central authority, retains the ultimate power to refine its rules.

If this Charter is adopted, modified, adapted, and improved by different communities in different contexts, that will be its highest purpose. It is offered not as a finished product, but as a starting point — a seed that can grow in diverse soils, shaped by the needs and wisdom of those who choose to use it.
