# FairCoin Community Charter
## (Official Version v1.0)

**Core Principle: Wealth lets you live better, but never at the expense of others.**

---

### Preamble

We, individuals and communities from diverse regions, cultures, and economic backgrounds, united by shared concern over the pervasive issues of monopoly, inequality, and trust deficits in human economic activities, voluntarily establish this Charter.

We believe:
- Labor is the source of value creation;
- Human dignity is the sole legitimate basis of governance power;
- Credit is the foundation of economic circulation;
- Wealth should be a tool for better living, not a means of controlling others.

We therefore establish the FairCoin Protocol as the foundational infrastructure for equitable global economic collaboration.

---

### Chapter I: General Provisions

**Article 1 (Definition)**  
FairCoin is a community-autonomous digital token issued on blockchain technology, with its value anchored to physical assets (grains, precious metals, and other commodities), governed strictly under the "One Person, One Vote" principle, and circulated based on members' credit redemption commitments.

**Article 2 (Non-Sovereign Status)**  
FairCoin does not belong to any nation, government, or international organization. The legality of FairCoin activities within any sovereign state is determined by that state's laws; this Charter does not interfere.

**Article 3 (Open Participation)**  
Any natural person or legally constituted organization, upon accepting all provisions of this Charter, is free to join or independently deploy a FairCoin sub-community.

---

### Chapter II: Core Principles

**Article 4 (Labor Creates Value)**  
FairCoin can only be obtained through genuine labor or lawful services. Any form of non-labor-based issuance, pre-sale, or airdrop (except initial basic activation) is prohibited. More labor yields more reward; no labor yields no reward.

**Article 5 (Personhood Determines Power)**  
FairCoin communities operate under a "One Person, One Vote" governance system. The number of tokens held, wealth, moral reputation, family background, or any other factor does not affect the equality of voting rights. Economic capacity and political power are permanently separated.

**Article 6 (Credit Supports Circulation)**  
Rights come with obligations. Every token holder is simultaneously a redemption guarantor, promising to fulfill redemption obligations when others present tokens for physical goods. The credit system constrains transactional behavior but does not affect basic holding or voting rights.

**Article 7 (Wealth Protection and Inheritance)**  
Legitimately held FairCoin is protected by the community. No member or organization may unlawfully deprive another of their FairCoin holdings. FairCoin may be inherited by legal heirs, but voting rights do not transfer with the tokens.

---

### Chapter III: Economic Mechanisms

**Article 8 (Basic Holding)**  
Each new member who passes identity verification receives a fixed amount of basic FairCoin free of charge. The initial amount of basic tokens is determined by the community's first governance vote.

**Article 9 (Issuance Mechanism)**  
FairCoin has no total supply cap. Issuance occurs only in the following cases:
- **Population issuance**: Upon each newly verified member, the system issues basic tokens at the current anchoring exchange rate.
- **Contribution issuance**: Upon completion of community-recognized labor, members receive tokens equivalent to the local market value of their labor.
- **Operational issuance**: Upon community approval of the platform's operational budget through voting, tokens are issued according to the budget.

All issuance must be accompanied by a proportional expansion of the physical credit reserve.

**Article 10 (Value Anchoring)**  
FairCoin's value is anchored to a basket of physical assets consisting of staple grains and precious metals (gold, silver, copper). The specific ratios are determined by each sub-community's own vote.

**Article 11 (Inter-Community Exchange Rate)**  
FairCoin of different sub-communities is exchanged through a decentralized exchange bridge at market-driven rates. Exchange rates are determined by the global market prices of the assets each community anchors to, free from manipulation by any central authority.

---

### Chapter IV: Governance Mechanism

**Article 12 (One Person, One Vote)**  
Major community affairs are decided by all members through "One Person, One Vote." Voting rights are independent of token holdings, wealth, credit standing, moral status, or any other factor.

**Article 13 (Scope of Voting)**  
The following matters are subject to community vote:
- Amendments to this Charter;
- Tax rates (if any) and operational budgets;
- Formulation and modification of the contribution activity list;
- Adjustment of reserve asset ratios;
- Changes to inter-community protocols;
- Dispute arbitration rules.

**Article 14 (Separation of Platform and Market)**  
The FairCoin Platform is solely responsible for system security, transaction verification, and governance execution. It does not participate in any commercial activities or resource allocation. Public services such as road and bridge construction are initiated freely by members, competitively bid, and operated at their own risk and profit.

---

### Chapter V: Credit and Redemption

**Article 15 (Credit Limit)**  
Each member receives an equal initial credit limit. This credit limit measures their redemption capacity and adjusts dynamically based on historical redemption records.

**Article 16 (Redemption Obligation)**  
No member may unreasonably refuse to redeem FairCoin held by others with physical goods or services. If refusal is confirmed by fact-checking, the community may vote to impose credit restrictions on that member's account.

**Article 17 (Anti-Free-rider Mechanism)**  
Accounts that have not engaged in any selling transaction for 90 consecutive days will see their credit limit gradually decrease to zero. After reaching zero, the account cannot use FairCoin to purchase goods until credit is re-established through labor or services.

**Article 18 (Inheritance)**  
Upon a member's death, their legitimate FairCoin holdings are inherited in full by their legal heirs. Heirs inherit the tokens but not the credit limit and must re-establish their credit record independently.

---

### Chapter VI: Platform Operations and Finance

**Article 19 (Operational Funding)**  
The FairCoin Platform does not levy any transaction tax or holding tax. Operational expenses (server maintenance, code audits, development upgrades, etc.) are funded through annual "operational issuance budgets" approved by the community vote.

**Article 20 (Platform Shutdown Mechanism)**  
If the operational budget is rejected by the community for two consecutive years, the platform shall enter an orderly shutdown procedure, with all assets returned to token holders and smart contract permissions transferred to a community-designated address.

---

### Chapter VII: Dispute Resolution and Arbitration

**Article 21 (Tiered Arbitration)**  
Internal community disputes shall first be resolved through consultation between the parties. If consultation fails, the dispute may be submitted to a community vote for ruling. If dissatisfied with the ruling, an appeal may be filed with an arbitration panel randomly selected for review.

**Article 22 (Arbitration Panel Formation)**  
Arbitration panel members are randomly selected by the system from among 10 members. Their ruling is final.

**Article 23 (Prohibition of Tyranny)**  
Any punitive vote must be based on factual evidence. The maximum penalty shall not exceed the direct economic loss caused by the wrongful act. The punished party has the right to appeal.

---

### Chapter VIII: Network Expansion and Final Provisions

**Article 24 (Open Protocol)**  
This Charter is an open protocol. Any organization or individual, upon accepting its principles, may independently deploy a FairCoin sub-community and become part of the FairCoin network.

**Article 25 (Amendment of Charter)**  
Amendments to this Charter require a vote of all members, with at least two-thirds in favor and a voter turnout exceeding 50%.

**Article 26 (Effective Date)**  
This Charter takes effect upon approval by the first community governance vote.

---

**We believe that fairness is not defined by a few, but grows from the practice of countless communities. You are welcome to be part of this practice.**
