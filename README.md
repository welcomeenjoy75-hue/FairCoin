## 中文版

### 核心理念

**财富让你自己活得更好，但不让别人活得更差。**

### 三大基石

1. **劳动创造价值** —— 公平币只能通过真实劳动或合法服务获得。不预售、不空投、不挖矿。
2. **人格决定权力** —— 社区治理实行一人一票，经济能力与政治权力永久分离。
3. **信用支撑流通** —— 权利即义务，持币即兑付承诺。拒绝兑付者被系统自然约束。

### 项目简介

公平币（FairCoin）是一份基于劳动、信用与一人一票原则的**全球公平经济协议**。

它不是加密货币投资项目，不是公司，不是金融产品。它是一套**开放的、可自行部署的社区自治规则**。

- 锚定实物（粮食与贵金属），天生抗通胀/通缩
- 二次方投票机制，防止多数人暴政
- 信用约束机制，拒绝兑付者自然被系统淘汰
- 多重宇宙结构，一人可同时参与多个社区，身份与信用独立
- 两成员即可运行，无需融资或政府批准

## English

### Core Principle

**Wealth lets you live better, but never at the expense of others.**

### Three Pillars

1. **Labor Creates Value** — FairCoin can only be obtained through real work or legitimate services. No pre-sales, no airdrops, no mining.
2. **Personhood Determines Power** — Governance follows "One Person, One Vote." Economic capacity and political power are permanently separated.
3. **Credit Supports Circulation** — Rights come with obligations. Holding coins means a commitment to redeem. Refusal to redeem is naturally constrained by the system.

### About FairCoin

FairCoin is a **global fair economy protocol** based on labor, credit, and one-person-one-vote governance.

It is not a cryptocurrency investment project, not a company, not a financial product. It is a set of **open, self-deployable community governance rules**.

- Anchored to physical goods (grains & precious metals) — inherently resistant to inflation and deflation
- Quadratic voting mechanism to prevent tyranny of the majority
- Credit constraint mechanism — those who refuse to redeem are naturally eliminated
- Multiverse structure — one person can join multiple communities with independent identities and credit
- Can be run by as few as two members — no financing or government approval required
